School

Structured learning with faith-connected daily life.

The school program combines curriculum, syllabi, lesson plans, and teacher training. It supports activity-based learning, storytelling, language development, child development, Qur'anic reflection, and Sunnah-centered habits.

5+ Years

Recognize Allah through creation, recite selected ayat, and build foundations in Arabic, English, Urdu, counting, colours, and shapes.

7+ Years

Strengthen reflection, begin reading and writing, understand Surah Al-Fatihah, salah words, and good and bad choices.

10+ Years

Develop awareness of Jannah, Islamic identity, good character, grammar, mathematics, and science through signs of Allah in creation.

12+ Years

Begin Hifz of the 30th Juz with understanding, Qur'anic Arabic analysis, deeper tadabbur, and independent application.

Steps to Grow

A monthly reading rhythm for the whole family.

Families read one carefully developed reader for a full month. The same story is explored in English, Urdu, and Arabic, helping children build language, focus, memory, imagination, and connection with Allah.

Week 1English bedtime reading
Week 2Urdu bedtime reading
Week 3Arabic bedtime reading
Final DaysThree days of revision in each language

Mindful Beginning

Practical life skills for calmer, healthier choices.

This course helps students, parents, professionals, teachers, couples, and community workers understand thoughts, emotions, habits, stress, communication, and relationships.

Healthy and balanced living

Build routines that support sleep, focus, emotional stability, productivity, and long-term wellbeing.

Emotional awareness

Notice thoughts and feelings early so you can respond calmly instead of reacting on impulse.

Communication and relationships

Practice respectful speaking, listening, boundaries, and conflict resolution in everyday life.

Practical life skills

Strengthen resilience, decision-making, self-discipline, and adaptability in real situations.

Parenting

Parenting is a skill set, not just instinct.

The parenting program helps adults understand child development, emotional regulation, healthy discipline, communication, behavior, online safety, and stress reduction.

Good parenting is less about perfection and more about responsiveness, consistency, and willingness to learn.

Qur'aan and Critical Thinking

Reflection, reason, and revelation working together.

This course helps learners engage with the Qur'aan thoughtfully: observing, questioning assumptions, evaluating information, recognizing bias, and applying Qur'aanic guidance to personal and social challenges.

Understanding revelation

Read with reflection, context, and awareness so guidance connects to real life, not just memorization.

Thinking clearly

Analyze ideas, compare evidence, and separate facts from assumptions, emotion, and misinformation.

Self-awareness

Reflect on intentions, habits, and reactions to strengthen humility, maturity, and responsibility.

Balanced dialogue

Learn how to discuss respectfully, disagree constructively, and apply wisdom with empathy.
